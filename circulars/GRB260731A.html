S.Salunke(IUCAA), Harsha K. H. (IUCAA), A. Goyal (IITB), A. Arya (IITB), G. Waratkar (Caltech/IITB), A. Vibhute (IUCAA), V. Bhalerao (IITB), D. Bhattacharya (Ashoka University/IUCAA), A. R. Rao (IUCAA/TIFR), and S. Vadawale (PRL) report on behalf of the AstroSat CZTI collaboration:

Analysis of AstroSat CZTI data with the CIFT framework (Sharma et al., 2021, JApA, 42, 73) showed the detection of a long GRB 260731A which was also detected by SVOM (Trigger Num. sb26073115) and Konus-Wind (IPN Notices).

The source was clearly detected in the CZT detectors in the 20-200 keV energy range. The light curve peaks at 2026-07-31 23:58:22.466 UTC. The measured peak count rate associated with the burst is 314 (+34, -33) counts/s above the background in the combined data of three quadrants (out of four), with a total of 2019 (+162, -264) counts. The local mean background count rate was 202 (+4, -3) counts/s. We measure a T90 of 11 (+3, -3) s from the cumulative CZT light curve. 

The source was also clearly detected in the CsI anticoincidence (Veto) detector in the 100-500 keV energy range. The light curve peaks at 2026-07-31 23:58:21.203 UTC. The measured peak count rate associated with the burst is 417 (+49, -5) counts/s above the background in the combined data of all quadrants, with a total of 1801 (+233, -234) counts. The local mean background count rate was 591 (+3, -4) counts/s.
We measure a T90 of 8 (+4, -2) s from the cumulative Veto light curve.

CZTI data products like interactive and downloadable light curves for this GRB can be found at:https://astrosat.iucaa.in/cift/cift_products/ET20260731T235830/ET20260731T235830_details.html
