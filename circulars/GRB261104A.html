GCN Circular 46150: GRB 261104A: SVOM detection of burst

Event ID: GRB 261104A
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-04 21:03:59 UTC
Source file: 46150.json

M. G. Bernardini (INAF/LUPM), M. Gnaoui (IAP), and R. Hamburg (USRA) report on behalf of the SVOM/ECLAIRs team

At 2026-11-04T20:36:44 UTC (T0), SVOM/ECLAIRs triggered and located the gamma-ray burst GRB 261104A (SVOM burst-id sb26110402).

The following trigger information was received on the ground with low latency by the SVOM VHF Alert Network.

The burst was detected both by the Count-Rate Trigger (CRT) and the Image Trigger (IMT), which produced a sequence of 5 alerts. IMT provided the alert with the best signal-to-noise-ratio in the image (SNR) of 8.02 in the [5-20] keV energy band over a time window of 20.48 seconds starting at 2026-11-04T20:36:31.

The localization of the best alert is R.A., Dec. 6.2999, -54.4612 degrees:
R.A. (J2000) = 0h25m11.98s
Dec. (J2000) = -54d27m40.50s
 with a 90% confidence level (C.L.) radius of 9.81 arcmin (including systematic error of 2 arcmin added in quadrature).

The SVOM/ECLAIRs light curve showed a single broad peak structure with a T90 duration of about 40 s.

SVOM slewed to the burst.

MXT and VT began observing the field after the slew. The analysis of the data will be published in a future circular.

The Space-based multi-band astronomical Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA), French Space Agency (CNES), and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. SVOM/ECLAIRs was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IRAP, CNRS-APC. SVOM/GRM was developed by the Institute of High Energy Physics (IHEP) of CAS. SVOM/MXT was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IJCLab, University of Leicester, MPE.

GCN Circular 46154: GRB 261104A: LCO optical upper limits

Event ID: GRB 261104A
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-05 01:30:27 UTC
Source file: 46154.json

S.J. Yin(GXU),D. Turpin,  A. Saccardi , J. T. Palmerio (CEA/Irfu),Y.X. Guan(GXU), C. Wu , L.P. Xin,(NAOC), Y.Y. Shi(GXU), X.H. Han(NAOC), M. Gnaoui (IAP), L.Zhang (IHEP), A. Li (BNU),
 report on behalf of the SVOM mission team:

We observed the field of GRB 261104A (detected by SVOM burst-id sb26110402
 (Bernardini et al., GCN 46150) with the LCO 1m telescope at South Afican Astronomical Observatory equipped with the Sinistro instrument.

Our observation started on 2026-11-04T21:51:12.583 UTC (about 1.24 hr after the trigger) and we obtained 3x200 s exposures in the SDSS r filter.

In the stacked image, we do not detect any new source within the SVOM/ECLAIRs error box. We measure the following upper limit calibrated against the DESI DR10 catalog, and not corrected for Galactic extinction:

r > 22.31 AB (5-sigma, mid-time 1.33 hr after the trigger).

This project is funded by the SVOM collaboration.

Palmerio (CEA/Irfu), M. G. Bernardini (INAF/LUPM), M. Gnaoui (IAP) and R. Hamburg (USRA) report on behalf of the SVOM/VT team.

SVOM/VT performed an automatic slew on the burst GRB 261104A triggered by SVOM/ECLAIRs (sb26110402, Bernardini et al., GCN 46150). The observation started at 2026-11-04T21:31:59 UTC, about 0.92 hr in VT_B (400nm-650nm) and VT_R (650nm-1000nm) channels simultaneously.
 
No credible uncatalogued candidates were identified the stacked images within the ECLAIRs  error box (Bernardini et al., GCN 46150), compared with the Legacy Survey.

Preliminary upper limits are estimated in AB magnitudes and are not corrected for Galactic extinction:

Mid time | Band | Exposure Time |  5-sigma upper limit
1.10 h     VT_B      50*20 s       > 22.5 mag    
1.10 h     VT_R      50*18 s       > 22.5 mag

Our non-detection is consistent with the reports from LCO (Yin et al., GCN 46154).

GCN Circular 46163: GRB 261104A: SVOM/ECLAIRs refined analysis

Event ID: GRB 261104A
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-05 17:29:51 UTC
Source file: 46163.json

R. Hamburg (USRA), M. Brunet (IRAP), F. Piron (LUPM), M.-G. Bernardini (INAF/LUPM), and M. Gnaoui (IAP) report on behalf of the SVOM/ECLAIRs team:

Using the event-by-event data downloaded through the X-band ground station, we report further analysis of SVOM/ECLAIRs observations of GRB 261104A (SVOM burst-id sb26110402 – Bernardini et al. 2026, GCN 46150, trigger time T0 = 2026-11-04T20:36:44 UTC).

The burst that triggered ECLAIRs onboard shows a single peak lightcurve with most of the emission seen below 20 keV. The burst duration is T90 = 20.1 +3.3/-3.8 s in the 4-120 keV energy band. 

The time-averaged spectrum from T0+7.6 s to T0+27.5 s in the 5-120 keV energy range is best fitted by a power law model with a photon index of -1.76 +/- 0.19. With this model, the 4-120 keV fluence is (1.38 ± 0.43)e-7 erg/cm^2 and the 4-120 keV photon flux is (2.55 ± 0.45)e-1 ph/cm^2/s. 

All the quoted errors are at the 68% confidence level.
