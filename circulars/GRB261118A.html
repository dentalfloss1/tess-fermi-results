The Fermi GBM team reports the detection of a likely SHORT GRB

At 13:17:49 UT on 18 Nov 2026, the Fermi Gamma-ray Burst Monitor (GBM) triggered and located GRB 261118A (trigger 816700674.004288 / 261118554).

The on-ground calculated location, using the Fermi GBM trigger data, is RA = 172.9, Dec = 51.0 (J2000 degrees, equivalent to J2000 11h 31m, 51d 00'), with a statistical uncertainty of 7.7 degrees.

The angle from the Fermi LAT boresight is 165.0 degrees.
