GCN Circular 46331: EP261122a: Einstein Probe detection of an X-ray transient

Event ID: EP261122a
Submitter: EP Team at NAOC/CAS <ep_ta@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-22 09:20:39 UTC
Source file: 46331.json

Shuqi Zheng, Jinrui Liu, Yijing Yi(BNU), Haowei Peng(THU), Guojiong Yang and Haiwu Pan (NAOC, CAS) on behalf of the Einstein Probe (EP) team:

We report on the detection of an X-ray transient by the Wide-field X-ray Telescope (WXT) on board the Einstein Probe (EP) mission, designated EP261122a. The transient triggered EP-WXT (ID: 01799322834) at 2026-11-22T06:12:37 (UTC). The WXT position of the source is R.A. = 49.298 deg, DEC = 16.456 deg (J2000) with an uncertainty of 3 arcmin in radius (90% C.L. statistical and systematic).

A follow-up observation with the Follow-up X-ray Telescope (FXT) was performed automatically. Within the WXT error circle, an uncatalogued X-ray source was detected at R.A. = 49.3112 deg, DEC = 16.4443 deg (J2000) with an uncertainty of 20 arcsec in radius (90% C.L. statistical and systematic).

Further information will be updated when the telemetry data is received.

Launched on January 9, 2024, EP is a space X-ray observatory to monitor the soft X-ray sky with X-ray follow-up capability (Yuan et al. 2022, Handbook of X-ray and Gamma-ray Astrophysics).

GCN Circular 46350: EP261122a: Near-infrared upper limits with MDM/MIRAGE

Event ID: EP261122a
Submitter: Geoffrey Mo at Caltech / Carnegie Observatories <gmo@mit.edu>
Created: 2026-11-24 01:08:34 UTC
Source file: 46350.json

Geoffrey Mo (Carnegie/Caltech), S. Manyan, K. De, V. Karambelkar, D. Schiminovich (Columbia U) report:

We observed the field of EP261122a (Zheng et al., GCN 46331) in the near-infrared J and Hs bands with the MDM InfraRed Astronomy inGaas Experiment (MIRAGE) instrument on the MDM 1.3 m telescope.

Observations began at 2026-11-22T10:03:48 (+3.86 hours after the EP trigger) in the J band and 2026-11-22T10:27:16 (+4.24 hours) in the Hs band, lasting 1080 s in J and 1620 s in Hs. We do not detect any new source within the FXT localization error circle, setting the following 5-sigma upper limits in AB magnitudes: J > 19.93, Hs > 19.45.

MIRAGE is a new robotic YJHs-band near-infrared imager for the MDM 1.3m telescope. We thank the Mt Cuba Astronomical Foundation for development of MIRAGE and the MDM Observatory staff for supporting the observations.
