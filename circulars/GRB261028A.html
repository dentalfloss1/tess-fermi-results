The Fermi GBM team reports the detection of a likely LONG GRB

"At 13:06:16.22 UT on 28 October 2026, the Fermi Gamma-ray Burst Monitor (GBM)
triggered and located GRB 261028A (trigger 814885581/261028546).
This trigger was initially classified as Below horizon by the flight software,
but is in fact a GRB.

The on-ground calculated location, using the Fermi GBM trigger data,
is RA = 61.09, Dec = -62.05 (J2000 degrees, equivalent to J2000 04h 04m 21.6s, -62d 03'),
with a statistical uncertainty of 11.52 degrees.

The angle from the Fermi LAT boresight is 52 degrees.
