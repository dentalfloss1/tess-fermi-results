GCN Circular 46137: GRB 261103A: SVOM/ECLAIRs detection of a burst through on-ground search

Event ID: GRB 261103A
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-03 11:24:02 UTC
Source file: 46137.json

M. Brunet (IRAP), Y.-H.Cheng (YNU), W.-L.Zhang (PMO) report on behalf of the SVOM/ECLAIRs team

The SVOM/ECLAIRs telescope detected a transient source, labelled GRB 261103A, starting at 2026-11-03T06:51:11 UTC (trigger time T0), through an on-ground search with the event-by-event data downloaded through the X-band ground station.

The burst was detected within several energy ranges and timescales. The best detection is obtained by the Image Trigger with a signal-to-noise ratio of 7.1 within 4-20 keV over a time window of 40.96 seconds starting at T0-40.96s.

The burst that triggered ECLAIRs onground shows a single peak lightcurve.

The localization of the source is RA, Dec = 14.157, 18.701 degrees:

RA (J2000) = 00h56m37.7s    
Dec (J2000) = 18d42m03.6s

with a 90% C.L. radius of 11.04 arcmin (including systematic error of 2 arcmin added in quadrature).

We note that no bright catalogued X-ray sources are found in the error box

All the quoted errors are at the 68% confidence level.

The Space-based multi-band astronomical Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA), French Space Agency (CNES), and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. ECLAIRs was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IRAP, CNRS-APC.

The Burst Advocate (BA) on shift for this alert is Y.-H.Cheng (yhcheng@mail.ynu.edu.cn)
Please contact the BA by email if you require additional information.

GCN Circular 46138: GRB 261103A: LCO optical upper limits

Event ID: GRB 261103A
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-03 15:00:08 UTC
Source file: 46138.json

Y.X. Guan(GXU), A. Saccardi (CEA/Irfu), S.J. Yin(GXU), C. Wu (NAOC), L.P. Xin, Y.Y. Shi(GXU), X.H. Han(NAOC), D. Turpin (CEA/Irfu), Y.-H.Cheng (YNU), W.-L.Zhang (PMO)
 report on behalf of the SVOM mission team:

We observed the field of GRB 261103A (detected by SVOM (Brunet et al., GCN 46137) with the LCO 1m telescope at Siding Spring Observatory equipped with the Sinistro instrument.

Our observation started on 2026-11-03T12:25:42.756 UT (about 5.58 hr after the trigger) and we obtained 3x200 s exposures in the SDSS r and 3x200 s exposures in the Pan-STARRS z filters.

In the stacked image, we do not detect any new source within the SVOM/ECLAIRs error box. We measure the following upper limit calibrated against the Pan-STARRS DR1 catalog, and not corrected for Galactic extinction:

r > 22.11 AB (5-sigma, mid-time 5.84 hr after the trigger);
z > 19.68 AB (5-sigma, mid-time 6.31 hr after the trigger).

This project is funded by the SVOM collaboration.

We observed the field of GRB 261103A detected by SVOM/ECLAIRs  (Brunet et al., GCN 46137) with LATIOS on SVOM/C-GFT. Observations started at 2026-11-03T 11:17:01.4 UTC, ~ 4.43 hr after the trigger.

We measure the following upper limit calibrated against the Pan-STARRS DR1 catalog, and not corrected for Galactic extinction:
 
i > 20.1AB (3-sigma, mid-time 4.58 hr after the trigger).
 
 
We thank the observation assistants Y.-H. Hao and C.-L. Guo  at Jilin observatory for their excellent support.
 
The Chinese Ground Follow-up Telescope (C-GFT) for the SVOM mission is located at Jilin Station, Changchun Observatory, National Astronomical Observatories, CAS. It features two instruments: (1) CATCH at the Cassegrain focus with a 21 arcmin x 21 arcmin FOV for simultaneous g/r/i-band imaging, and (2) LATIOS, a 4k x 4k CMOS camera at the prime focus with a 1.28 deg x 1.28 deg FOV that images in g, r, and i bands via filter switching.

GCN Circular 46176: GRB 261103A: SVOM/ECLAIRs refined analysis

Event ID: GRB 261103A
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-06 17:56:10 UTC
Source file: 46176.json

M. Brunet (IRAP), Y.-H.Cheng (YNU), W.-L.Zhang (PMO) report on behalf of the SVOM/ECLAIRs team

Using the event-by-event data downloaded through the X-band ground station, we report further analysis of SVOM/ECLAIRs observations of the possible burst GRB 261103A (GCN 46137, trigger time T0 = 2026-11-03T06:51:11 UTC). 

The event that triggered ECLAIRs onground shows a single peak lightcurve. The burst duration is T90 = 35 +/- 13 s in the 4-20 keV energy band.

The time-averaged spectrum from T0-33.8 s to T0+0.8 s in the 5-120 keV energy range is best fitted by a powerlaw model with a photon index alpha = -3 +0.5/-0.8. With this model, the 4-120 keV fluence is (1.4 +/- 0.4)e-7 erg/cm^2 and the 4-120 keV photon flux is 0.32 +/- 0.06 ph/cm^2/s. 

The spectrum can be equally fitted by a blackbody model with a temperature kT = 2.0 +0.7/-0.5 keV. With this model, the 4-120 keV fluence is (9.7 +/- 2.3)e-8 erg/cm^2 and the 4-120 keV photon flux is 0.23 +0.04/-0.05 ph/cm^2/s. 

If this event is a GRB, it can be classified as an X-Ray Flash (XRF).

All the quoted errors are at the 68% confidence level.

The Space-based multi-band astronomical Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA), French Space Agency (CNES), and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. ECLAIRs was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IRAP, CNRS-APC.

GCN Circular 46187: GRB 261103A: SVOM/VT optical upper limits

Event ID: GRB 261103A
Submitter: Huali Li at at NAOC, SVOM <lhl@nao.cas.cn>
Created: 2026-11-08 01:56:52 UTC
Source file: 46187.json

H. L. Li, Y. N. Ma, C. Wu, L. P. Xin, Y. L. Qiu, Z. H. Yao, J. R. Xu, L. J. Chen, X. H. Han, J. Wang, Y. Xu, P. P. Zhang, W. J. Xie, Y. J. Xiao, H. B. Cai, J. S. Deng, J. Y. Wei (NAOC), J. T. Palmerio (CEA/Irfu), Y. H.Cheng (YNU), W. L.Zhang (PMO) report on behalf of the SVOM/VT team.

SVOM/VT performed a ToO observation on the burst GRB 261103A triggered by SVOM/ECLAIRs (Brunet et al., GCN 46137, 46176). The observation started at 2026-11-03T11:59:33 UTC, about 5.15 hr in VT_B (400nm-650nm) and VT_R (650nm-1000nm) channels simultaneously.
 
No credible uncatalogued candidates were identified in the stacked images within the ECLAIRs  error box (Bernardini et al., GCN 46150), compared with the Legacy Survey, down to 22.0 mag in AB magnitudes, at the mid-time of 6.9 hr post trigger.

Our non-detection is consistent with the reports from LCO (Guan et al., GCN 46138) and SVOM/C-GFT (Yin et al.., GCN 46157).

The Space Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA, China), National Center for Space Studies (CNES, France) and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS, China), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. VT was jointly developed by Xi'an Institute of Optics and Precision Mechanics (XIOPM), CAS and National astronomical observatories (NAOC), CAS.
