GCN Circular 46336: GRB 261122B: SVOM detection of a burst

Event ID: GRB 261122B
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-22 23:21:00 UTC
Source file: 46336.json

M. G. Bernardini (INAF-OAB, LUPM), S. Antier (IJCLAB), M. Dinatolo (INAF-OAB), Hatsune Goto (CEA/RIKEN) report on behalf of the SVOM mission team:
At 2026-11-22T22:46:55 UTC (T0), SVOM/ECLAIRs triggered and located the gamma-ray burst GRB 261122B (SVOM burst-id sb26112202).

The following trigger information was received on the ground with low latency by the SVOM VHF Alert Network.

The burst was detected both by the Count-Rate Trigger (CRT) and the Image Trigger (IMT), which produced a sequence of 24 alerts. CRT provided the alert with the best signal-to-noise-ratio in the image (SNR) of 22.45 in the [8-120] keV energy band over a time window of 5.10 seconds starting at 2026-11-22T22:46:52.

The localization of the best alert is R.A., Dec. 3.9375, -38.8029 degrees (J2000) with a 90% confidence level (C.L.) radius of 3.97 arcmin (including systematic error of 2 arcmin added in quadrature).

This burst also triggered SVOM/GRM at 2026-11-22T22:46:54 on a timescale of 0.10 seconds with an SNR of 6.90.

SVOM slewed to the burst.

SVOM/MXT began observing the field at 2026-11-22T22:49:45 UTC, 170 seconds after T0. Using onboard processed data we found an uncatalogued X-ray source located at R.A., Dec. 3.9789, -38.8248 degrees:
R.A. (J2000) = 0h15m54.93s
Dec. (J2000) = -38d49m29.40s
with a 90% C.L. radius of 29.15 arcseconds.

This location is 2.34 arcminutes from the ECLAIRs onboard position. 
This position may be improved as more data is received.

VT began observing the field after the slew. The analysis of the data will be published in a future circular.

The Space-based multi-band astronomical Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA), French Space Agency (CNES), and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. SVOM/ECLAIRs was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IRAP, CNRS-APC. SVOM/GRM was developed by the Institute of High Energy Physics (IHEP) of CAS. SVOM/MXT was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IJCLab, University of Leicester, MPE.

The Burst Advocate (BA) on shift for this alert is Sarah Antier: antier@ijclab.in2p3.fr.
Please contact the BA by email if you require additional information.

GCN Circular 46340: GRB 261122B: NOT optical counterpart confirmation

Event ID: GRB 261122B
Submitter: Gregory Corcoran at University College Dublin <gregory.corcoran@ucdconnect.ie>
Created: 2026-11-23 00:30:53 UTC
Source file: 46340.json

G. Corcoran (UCD), D. B. Malesani (DAWN/NBI and Radboud U.), A. Martin-Carrillo (UCD), O. Stalder (NOT), M. R. Alexandersen (NOT) report on behalf of a larger collaboration

We observed the field of the SVOM/ECLAIRs GRB 261122B (Bernardini et al., GCNs 46336, 46339) using the Nordic Optical Telescope (NOT) equipped with the ALFOSC instrument. Observations were performed starting at 23:08:10 UT on 22 November 2026, and consisted of 3x300 s in the SDSS-i band.

We detect the optical counterpart discovered by SVOM/VT (Wu et al., GCN 46338) for which we measure an AB magnitude of:

i = 18.33 +/- 0.06 (midtime: 29.2 minutes post trigger).

Our photometry was calibrated using nearby stars from the Legacy Survey and is not corrected for Galactic extinction.

We observed the field of the SVOM/ECLAIRs GRB 261122B (Bernardini et al., GCN 46336,46339) with the LCO 1m telescope at South African Astronomical Observatory equipped with the Sinistro instrument.
 
Our observation started on 2026-11-22 at 22:51:01.5 UTC and we obtained 3x200 s exposures in the SDSS r and 3x200 s exposures in the Pan-STARRS z filters.
 
The optical counterpart (Wu et al., GCN 46338; Corcoran et al., GCN 46340 ) is detected in our images. We measure the following magnitudes calibrated against the DESI DR10 catalog, and not corrected for Galactic extinction:
 
r = 17.84 +/- 0.01 AB (mid-time 9.5 min after the trigger);
z = 17.35 +/- 0.02 AB (mid-time 11.2 min after the trigger).
 
This project is funded by the SVOM collaboration.

We imaged the field of the SVOM GRB 261122B (Bernardini et al., GCN Circ. 46336, 46339) using the DDRAGO wide-field imager on the SVOM/COLIBRÍ (FM-GFT) telescope. We observed from 2026-11-23 02:05:28 to 02:36:18 UTC (from 3.26 to 3.79 hours after the trigger) and obtained 26 minutes of exposure in the r filter.

The data were reduced and coadded with the ASU pipeline. The photometry was calibrated using nearby stars from the PanSTARRS DR1 catalog, is in the AB system, and is not corrected for Galactic extinction.

We detected the optical counterpart reported by Wu et al., GCN 46338; Corcoran et al., GCN 46340, Yin et al., 46341, at a preliminary magnitude of:

r = 20.01 +/- 0.03. 

Further observations are ongoing.

We thank the staff of the Observatorio Astronómico Nacional at Sierra de San Pedro Mártir, as well as the technical and engineering teams at CEA, CPPM, IRAP, LAM, OHP, OSU Pytheas, and UNAM.

COLIBRÍ is an astronomical observatory developed and operated jointly by France (AMU, CNES and CNRS) and Mexico (UNAM and SECIHTI). It is located at the Observatorio Astronómico Nacional on the Sierra de San Pedro Mártir, Baja California, Mexico.

With the limited X-band data available, the optical counterpart (Wu et al., GCN 46338; Corcoran et al., GCN 46340; Yin et al., GCN 46341; Globus et al., GCN 46342) was clearly detected in single frame of both channels. It exhibits a power-law decay with a slope of -0.6 over our 3.4-hour observation span. The following measurements are in AB magnitudes and are not corrected for Galactic extinction:

| Mid time(t-T0) | Band | Exposure Time | Brightness         |
| -------------- | ---- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| 356 s          | VT_B | 70 s          | 18.11 +/- 0.06 mag |
| 356 s          | VT_R | 70 s          | 17.41 +/- 0.06 mag |

More observations are ongoing.

We observed the field of GRB 261122B detected by SVOM/ECLAIRs and GRM (Bernardini et al., GCN 46336) with the GRANDMA citizen science project Kilonova-catcher (KNC). GCN Circular 46351: GRB 261122B: ATCA Detection of Radio Counterpart

Event ID: GRB 261122B
Submitter: u6074340@umail.utah.edu
Created: 2026-11-24 01:32:51 UTC
Source file: 46351.json

A. Gulati (UUtah), G. E. Anderson (Curtin),  S. Chastain (UNM),  A. J. van der Horst (GWU), J. K. Leung (UofT/HUJI), and L. Rhodes (TSI/McGill) on behalf of the ATCA PanRadio GRB collaboration

We observed SVOM GRB 261122B (Bernardini et al., GCN 46336) with the Australia Telescope Compact Array (ATCA) at multiple frequencies 6.6 hours post-burst.

In our preliminary analysis, we detect a radio counterpart at 5 GHz with a flux density of ~0.2 mJy at a position consistent with the optical counterpart position (Wu et al., GCN 46338). Further observations are planned.
