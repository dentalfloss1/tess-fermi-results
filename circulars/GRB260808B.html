GCN Circular 45316: GRB 260808B: SVOM detection of a faint burst

Event ID: GRB 260808B
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-08-08 15:12:17 UTC
Source file: 45316.json

Laurent Bouchet (IRAP), Sebastien Guillot (IRAP), Michela Dinatolo (U. Milano), Diego Götz (CEA)  report on behalf of the SVOM mission team:

At 2026-08-08T14:20:58 UTC (T0), SVOM/ECLAIRs triggered and located the gamma-ray burst GRB 260808# (SVOM burst-id sb26080812).

The following trigger information was received on the ground with low latency by the SVOM VHF Alert Network.

The burst was only detected by the Image Trigger (IMT), which produced a sequence of 5 alerts. IMT provided the alert with the best signal-to-noise-ratio in the image (SNR) of 7.52 in the [5-20] keV energy band over a time window of 40.96 seconds starting at 2026-08-08T14:20:18.

The localization of the best alert is R.A., Dec. 345.2202, 8.4310 degrees (J2000) with a 90% confidence level (C.L.) radius of 10.44 arcmin (including systematic error of 2 arcmin added in quadrature).

SVOM slewed to the burst.

SVOM/MXT began observing the field at 2026-08-08T14:23:30 UTC, 152 seconds after T0. Using onboard processed data we found an uncatalogued X-ray source located at R.A., Dec. 345.239, 8.467 degrees:
R.A. (J2000) = 23h00m57.38s
Dec. (J2000) = 8d28m00.34s
with a 90% C.L. radius of 48 arcseconds.

This location is 2.42 arcminutes from the ECLAIRs onboard position. This position may be improved as more data is received.

VT began observing the field after the slew. The analysis of the data will be published in a future circular.

The Space-based multi-band astronomical Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA), French Space Agency (CNES), and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. SVOM/ECLAIRs was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IRAP, CNRS-APC. SVOM/GRM was developed by the Institute of High Energy Physics (IHEP) of CAS. SVOM/MXT was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IJCLab, University of Leicester, MPE.

The Burst Advocate (BA) on shift for this alert is Michela Maria Dinatolo: michela.dinatolo@unimi.it.

GCN Circular 45333: GRB 260808B: SVOM/ECLAIRs refined analysis

Event ID: GRB 260808B
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-08-09 19:10:45 UTC
Source file: 45333.json

Authors: M. Brunet, L. Bouchet, S. Guillot (IRAP), Michela Dinatolo (U. Milano)
report on behalf of the SVOM/ECLAIRs team

Using the event-by-event data downloaded through the X-band ground station, we report further analysis of SVOM/ECLAIRs observations of GRB 260808B (SVOM burst-id sb26080812 – GCN 45316, trigger time T0 = 2026-08-08T14:20:58 UTC).

The burst that triggered ECLAIRs onboard shows a multiple peak lightcurve with a possible precursor at T0-84.5 s lasting around 2 s. The burst duration is T90 = 178 +12/-8 s in the 4-120 keV energy band.

The time-averaged spectrum before the slew, from T0-45s to T0+43s in the 5-120 keV energy range is best fitted by a powerlaw model with alpha = -1.96 +0.14/-0.16. With this model, the 4-120 keV fluence is 6.4 +/-0.9 e-7  erg/cm^2 and the 4-120 keV photon flux is 0.32 +/-0.03 ph/cm^2/s. 

The possible precursor is too faint to extract any significant spectral parameters.

All the quoted errors are at the 68% confidence level.
The Space-based multi-band astronomical Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA), French Space Agency (CNES), and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. ECLAIRs was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IRAP, CNRS-APC.

A number of metal absorption features are detected, which we interpret as due to Fe II, Mg II and Mg I, all at a common redshift z = 1.295, which we suggest to be the redshift of GRB 260808B.
