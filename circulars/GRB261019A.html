The Fermi GBM team reports the detection of a likely LONG GRB.

"At 09:14:05.59 UT on 19 October 2026, the Fermi Gamma-ray Burst Monitor (GBM) triggered and located GRB 261017A (trigger 814094050/261019385).

The on-ground calculated location, using the Fermi GBM trigger data, is RA = 321.9, Dec = -72.4 (J2000 degrees, equivalent to J2000 21h 27m, -72d 24'), with a statistical uncertainty of 2.6 degrees.

The angle from the Fermi LAT boresight is 118 degrees.

The skymap can be found here: https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/FTP/fermi/data/gbm/triggers/2026/bn261019385/quicklook/glg_skymap_all_bn261019385.png
