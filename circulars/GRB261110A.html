GCN Circular 46215: GRB 261110A: SVOM detection of a burst

Event ID: GRB 261110A
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-10 12:19:20 UTC
Source file: 46215.json

Chen-Wei Wang (THU), Li Zhang (IHEP), Wen-Jun Tan (PKU), S. Schanne, A. Saccardi, B. Cordier, D. Turpin (CEA/Irfu) report on behalf of the SVOM mission team:

At 2026-11-10T11:50:00 UTC (T0), SVOM/ECLAIRs triggered and located the gamma-ray burst GRB 261110A (SVOM burst-id sb26111002).

The following trigger information was received on the ground with low latency by the SVOM VHF Alert Network.

The burst was only detected by the Image Trigger (IMT), which produced a sequence of 3 alerts. IMT provided the alert with the best signal-to-noise-ratio in the image (SNR) of 7.60 in the [5-20] keV energy band over a time window of 40.96 seconds starting at 2026-11-10T11:49:19.

The localization of the best alert is R.A., Dec. 350.7538, 33.0385 degrees:
R.A. (J2000) = 23h23m00.91s
Dec. (J2000) = 33d02m18.62s
 with a 90% confidence level (C.L.) radius of 10.33 arcmin (including systematic error of 2 arcmin added in quadrature).

The SVOM/ECLAIRs light curve showed a weak peak structure with a T90 duration of about 21.891 (-8.717 +8.734).

SVOM slewed to the burst.

MXT and VT began observing the field after the slew. The analysis of the data will be published in a future circular.

The Space-based multi-band astronomical Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA), French Space Agency (CNES), and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. SVOM/ECLAIRs was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IRAP, CNRS-APC. SVOM/GRM was developed by the Institute of High Energy Physics (IHEP) of CAS. SVOM/MXT was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IJCLab, University of Leicester, MPE.

The Burst Advocate (BA) on shift for this alert is Wenjun Tan: tanwj@pku.edu.cn.
Please contact the BA by email if you require additional information.

z > 20.2 AB (3-sigma, mid-time 13.07 hr after the trigger).

GCN Circular 46234: GRB 261110A: SVOM/ECLAIRs refined analysis

Event ID: GRB 261110A
Submitter: SVOM_group <svomgroup@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-12 08:18:22 UTC
Source file: 46234.json

N. Dagoneau (CEA), F. Piron (LUPM), Chen-Wei Wang (THU) and Wen-Jun Tan (PKU) report on behalf of the SVOM/ECLAIRs team

Using the event-by-event data downloaded through the X-band ground station, we report further analysis of SVOM/ECLAIRs observations of GRB 261110A (SVOM burst-id sb26111002, GCN 46215, trigger time T0 = 2026-11-10T11:50:00 UTC). 

The burst that triggered ECLAIRs onboard shows a single broad peak lightcurve. The burst duration is T90 = 32 -5/+8 s in the 4-120 keV energy band.

The time-averaged spectrum from T0-31.84 s to T0-0.32 s in the 5-120 keV energy range is best fitted by a power-law model with a power-law index alpha = -2.01 ± 0.20. With this model, the 4-120 keV fluence is (2.36 ± 0.48)e-7 erg/cm² and the 4-120 keV photon flux is (3.01 ± 0.61)e-1 ph/cm²/s. 

All the quoted errors are at the 68% confidence level.

The Space-based multi-band astronomical Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA), French Space Agency (CNES), and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. ECLAIRs was developed jointly by CNES, CEA-IRFU, CNRS-IRAP, CNRS-APC.
