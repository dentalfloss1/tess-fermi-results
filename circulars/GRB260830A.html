GCN Circular 45483: GRB 260830A: NuSTAR detection of prompt emission

Event ID: GRB 260830A
Submitter: Gaurav Waratkar at Caltech <gauravwaratkar@iitb.ac.in>
Created: 2026-09-01 07:06:48 UTC
Source file: 45483.json

G. Waratkar (Caltech) and B. Grefenstette (Caltech) report on behalf of the NuSTAR Search for INteresting Gamma-ray Signals (SINGS) working group:

The NuSTAR SINGS working group reports the detection of prompt emission from the long-duration GRB 260830A in both the NuSTAR CsI anti-coincidence shields. Details of the search algorithm will be described in a future paper.

The NuSTAR SINGS algorithm, triggered at 2026-08-30T19:32:13.02 UTC, shows a detection of GRB 260830A, also detected by Konus-Wind (GCN notices).

The NuSTAR CsI shield data are recorded at 1 Hz. We detect a single episode with two peaks lasting for about 10-s. The peak count rate is ~1500-cps with a baseline rate of ~1000-cps during this time period. We do not see any evidence in the signal above 100 keV in the CZT detectors.
