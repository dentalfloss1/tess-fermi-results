GCN Circular 46282: EP261115a: Einstein Probe detection of an X-ray transient

Event ID: EP261115a
Submitter: EP Team at NAOC/CAS <ep_ta@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-15 01:46:44 UTC
Source file: 46282.json

Y. Wang (PMO; UCB), Y. J. Yi (BNU), C. K. Kan (HKU), H. Sun (NAO, CAS) on behalf of the Einstein Probe (EP) team:

We report on the detection of an X-ray transient by the Wide-field X-ray Telescope (WXT) on board the Einstein Probe (EP) mission, designated EP261115a. The transient triggered EP-WXT (ID: 01709322538) at 2026-11-15T00:22:13 (UTC). The WXT position of the source is R.A. = 58.644 deg, DEC = -44.311 deg (J2000) with an uncertainty of 3 arcmin in radius (90% C.L. statistical and systematic).

A follow-up observation with the Follow-up X-ray Telescope (FXT) was performed automatically. Within the WXT error circle, an uncatalogued X-ray source was detected at R.A. = 58.6217 deg, DEC = -44.2950 deg (J2000) with an uncertainty of 20 arcsec in radius (90% C.L. statistical and systematic).

Further information will be updated when the telemetry data is received.

Launched on January 9, 2024, EP is a space X-ray observatory to monitor the soft X-ray sky with X-ray follow-up capability (Yuan et al. 2022, Handbook of X-ray and Gamma-ray Astrophysics).

GCN Circular 46297: EP261115a: SVOM/VT optical upper limits

Event ID: EP261115a
Submitter: Yinuo Ma <mayn@bao.ac.cn>
Created: 2026-11-15 15:57:46 UTC
Source file: 46297.json

Y. N. Ma, H. L. Li, L. P. Xin, Y. L. Qiu, C. Wu, Z. H. Yao, J. R. Xu, L. J. Chen, X. H. Han, Y. Xu, J. Wang, P. P. Zhang, W. J. Xie, Y. J. Xiao, H. B. Cai, J. S. Deng, J. Y. Wei (NAOC), J. Palmerio (CEA) report on behalf of the SVOM/VT team. 

SVOM/VT performed a Target of Opportunity observation of EP261115a detected by EP/WXT (Wang et al., GCN 46282). SVOM/VT began observing the field at 2026-11-15T01:59:00 UTC, 1.613 hours after the trigger, in the VT_B (400nm-650nm) and VT_R (650nm-1000nm) channels simultaneously. 

No uncatalogued candidate was identified in our stacked images within the error box provided by EP/FXT position (Wang et al., GCN 46282), compared to the Legacy Survey. 

The following measurements are in AB magnitudes and are not corrected for Galactic extinction:

Mid time | Band | Exposure Time |  3-sigma upper limit
2.43 hr  | VT_B |    30*50 s    |    > 23.6 mag
2.43 hr  | VT_R |    28*50 s    |    > 23.3 mag

Our results are consistent with MASTER (Segura et al., GCN 46286).

The Space Variable Objects Monitor (SVOM) is a China-France joint mission led by the Chinese National Space Administration (CNSA, China), National Center for Space Studies (CNES, France) and the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS, China), which is dedicated to observing gamma-ray bursts and other transient phenomena in the energetic universe. VT was jointly developed by Xi'an Institute of Optics and Precision Mechanics (XIOPM), CAS and National astronomical observatories (NAOC), CAS.

GCN Circular 46298: EP261115a: FRAM-Auger optical limits

Event ID: EP261115a
Submitter: Martin Jelinek at Astro.Inst-AVCR,Ondrejov <martin.jelinek@asu.cas.cz>
Created: 2026-11-15 22:47:40 UTC
Source file: 46298.json

Martin Jelinek, Jan Strobl and Filip Novotny (ASU CAS Ondrejov, CZ),
Martin Masek, Sergey Karpov, Petr Janecek, Jakub Jurysek, Jan Ebr, Ronan Cunniffe, Petr Travnicek and Michael Prouza (Institute of Physics, Prague, CZ)

report:

The 30cm robotic telescope FRAM-Auger at the Pierre Auger Observatory (Cerro Los Leones, Malargue, Argentina) reacted robotically to the Einstein Probe WXT alert of EP261115a (Wang et al., GCN 46282), obtaining a series of 48 R-band images of 60 s each, starting at 01:25:27 UT, i.e. ~63 min after the trigger and ~2 min after the alert was received, and continuing until ~2.0 h after the trigger. The field was at an elevation of ~49-59 deg.

We do not detect any new source within the EP/FXT error circle, neither in single images nor in the combined frame, when comparing to the ATLAS-RefCat2 and Legacy Survey DR10 catalogues. We obtain the following limits:

T-T0[mid]  Images  Limit (Vega)
~64 min    1       R_C > 17.0
~92 min    48      R_C > 18.9

Single images reach R_C ~ 17.0 throughout the series. The two brightest red dwarf candidates within the WXT error circle (r ~ 16.2 and 16.9, both outside the FXT error circle) show no brightening beyond ~0.2 mag during our observations. Our limits are consistent with those of MASTER (Lipunov et al., GCN 46286) and SVOM/VT (Ma et al., GCN 46297).

The photometry is calibrated against the Atlas-REFCAT2 catalogue (Tonry et al. 2018, ApJ, 867, 105) and is not corrected for Galactic extinction.
